About CampusEats

CampusEats is a lightweight online food ordering platform built for university students, so a good meal is never more than a few taps away.

What is CampusEats?

A simple web-based food ordering system where students browse a menu of meals and drinks, add what they fancy to a cart, and place an order in minutes. Every dish belongs to a clearly labelled category, prices come straight from the menu, and a curated Nigerian collection sits alongside a live international feed pulled from TheMealDB on load.

Order history is stored so students can always look back at what they ordered, when, and how much it cost. It runs entirely in the browser, with data persisted locally — no server to maintain, no app to install.

Why we built it

Ordering between lectures on campus usually means long phone queues, orders taken down by ear, and no proof of what was ordered or when. That process is slow, easy to get wrong, and leaves no trail for the student or the vendor.

CampusEats replaces the phone call with a browser: browse a menu at your own pace, build a cart, pay-attention checkout with delivery details, and keep a record of every order — all in one lightweight page.
